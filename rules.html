GUHSIP rules

GUHSIP is a place for gist, news and conversation. Anonymous does not mean consequence-free.

Don't post

  • Threats, harassment, bullying or hate
  • Nudes or sexual content
  • Anyone's private details (doxxing)
  • Rumors that target named students, staff or private people
  • Anything illegal

Who can join

You must be 18 or older.

Reports and removal

Use Report on any post. Posts with 3 reports are hidden until they are reviewed. Posts that break these rules are removed, and accounts that keep breaking them are banned.

What we store

Your username, a scrambled version of your password, and what you post. We don't collect your email, name or phone number.

Your password

There is no password reset. If you forget your password, the account can't be recovered. Let your browser or password manager save it.

Responsibility

You are responsible for what you post. GUHSIP is not responsible for what users post, but we act quickly on reports.
