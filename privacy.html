Privacy policy

What we collect

Your email address, your username, a scrambled version of your password, and what you post, comment, react to and message. If you add them, your profile photo, bio and links. We also count how many people view each sip.

Who sees it

Other users only see your username, photo, bio, links and posts. Your email is private. Only GUHSIP admins can see it, and we use it to run the service, handle reports, and answer legal requests. We may share it with authorities if the law requires. Direct messages are visible only to you and the person you message. GUHSIP can review them if you report them or the law requires.

Who helps us run GUHSIP

We use Supabase to store data and handle logins, and a hosting provider to serve the website. They process data for us under their own terms.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account yourself from Edit profile on your profile page. That removes your posts, comments and messages.

Age

GUHSIP is for people aged 18 and older.

Your rights

Under Nigerian data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. Email us and we will respond.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here.
